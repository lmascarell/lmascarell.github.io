Laura Mascarell.

/로라

I am a postdoctoral researcher in Professor Sundong Kim's lab at the Gwangju Institute of Science and Technology (GIST) in South Korea, supported by the Research Group for Bio-Embodied Physical AI.

My research focuses on how agents reason and adapt in unfamiliar environments using ARC-AGI-3, contributing to broader questions about generalization and open-ended learning.

Previously, I was a postdoctoral researcher at ETH Zurich. I have a background in computer science and hold a PhD in computational linguistics from the University of Zurich.
